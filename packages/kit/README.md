# @keystone/kit

The app runtime every module and connector builds on. It depends on `@keystone/core` and `@keystone/ui` only and knows
no module and no connector (`npm run check:boundaries`). Parts import it by name: `import { useModuleApp } from
'@keystone/kit'`.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `src/registry.ts` | `ModuleDef`, `ModuleApp`, and `buildRegistry`, which validates parts, keeps one provider per service, and leaves out anything broken, with a reason. `findService` looks a service up by its published name |
| `src/store.ts` | Holds the saved state, runs rules (`run`: a refusal comes back as its sentence and the fields it names), writes `state.json` about 250ms after a burst of changes, never overwrites a damaged file until something changes, and runs modules' `onOpen` housekeeping |
| `src/context.tsx` | Hooks: `useKeystone` (the state, `ctx`, `run`, which returns the state after the rule, and the capabilities a screen may use: `files`, `photos`, `backup`; never the store or the platform), `useModuleApp` (`go`, `has`, `setDraft`, `service`), `useDraft`, `useOpenConnector` (opens a connected system's app under a 15-second deadline and says what happened), `usePhoto` |
| `src/Shell.tsx` | The rail, ⌘/Ctrl+number navigation, and one error boundary per screen |
| `src/ComingSoon.tsx` | `comingSoon`: a module as a screen not released yet (docs/adr/0017, 0019), marked "Soon" in the rail and "Coming soon" on its Home tile; its own screen shows faded behind a "Coming soon" notice, `inert` and hidden from screen readers. It keeps everything it contributes but its finding |
| `src/Session.tsx` | Signing out of the records in the cloud (docs/adr/0012): `SignOutButton` in the rail, and `IdleSignOut`, which asks "Still there?" a minute before and signs out once Keystone has sat unused for the session's `idleMs`. Nothing without `Platform.session` |
| `src/App.tsx` | The provider tree, used by the app's `main.tsx` and by its tests |
| `src/components.tsx` | Building blocks for screens: `LineEditor` (amounts, or `pricing="unit"` for quantity × unit price; its cells named `lines.<index>.<desc|qty|amount>` for a refusal to mark; Enter in a filled-in line moves to the next, adding one after the last, and in an unfinished line to its empty box, never submitting the form), `Workspace` (the form beside its document; side by side, the preview is pinned by default: it stays in view while the form scrolls, and a paper taller than the window scrolls in its own box. Pin preview, shown side by side only, unpins it to scroll with the page and show the whole paper; the choice is one for every screen's preview, kept with the drafts while Keystone is open; while a field the form wraps in `Fills` has focus, it marks the part of the paper that field fills and scrolls the pinned preview to it, docs/adr/0042), `FormCard` (its `error`, a refused outcome, marks the fields it names and focuses the first, docs/adr/0044), `ConnectorShortcut` (a connected system's logo, which opens it, and its app's newer version under it, asked when it shows and every hour, docs/adr/0035), `Feed`, `BankAccountPicker` (a company bank account from the bank-accounts service, with Others… to add one and Remove…; a voucher's Bank and a statement's Pay into), `SignatoryPicker` (a signatory's name, with Other… to type one: once Company Details keeps signatures, from whose they are, as kept, so whoever is picked signs; before then, or with `anyone` (whose signature to add), from the directory's employees, Company Details' names, whoever is signed in and the names on the module's own documents; every signatory on vouchers, statements, IDs and Company Details) |
| `src/drafts.tsx` | Drafts on a form (docs/adr/0041): `useSavedDrafts` (the form's draft saves itself and is written at most `AUTOSAVE_MS`, a second, after an edit, however long the typing goes on, so a force close loses no more, and at once when the form, the screen or Keystone is left; save it now, open one again, delete one, and finish it when the form is saved for real; a module says when its form is `blank`, and `private` keeps them to their author), `SaveDraftButton`, and `SavedDrafts`, the list above a register, each marked Draft with Open and Delete…. The drafts themselves are core's (`saveDraft`, `savedDrafts`, `removeDraft`), kept in the cloud as `drafts` and `mydrafts` |
| `src/paper.ts` | The paper, written once: an A4 page and its 12 mm print margin (`PAPER`, `PRINTABLE`, and the pixel sizes worked out from them), the marks a paper carries (`MARKS`), and one definition per layout (`UPRIGHT`, `LANDSCAPE_PAGE`, `TWO_UP`): its width, its `@page` rule, how it is fitted to the page before printing and put back after, and how its PDF page is set up. A kit test holds the print stylesheet to it |
| `src/documents.tsx` | Building blocks for printed documents. Three sheets, each a layout from `paper.ts`, always laid out at the paper's width and scaled down on screen to fit the column, so the preview, the print and the PDF are one layout: `Sheet` (upright, inside the margins), `LandscapePage` (the A4 on its side, for a sheet too wide to stand upright, printed scaled to one page) and `TwoUp` (the document twice on a whole A4, a copy on each half 6 mm in from the edges, with a dashed line across the middle to cut along). `Letterhead`, every paper's header, the same on each (docs/adr/0038): the title across the top, then the Group of Companies logo made for paper (`@keystone/ui/logo-paper-companies.png`) with the company's built-in address beside it, and the number and date on the right. `Meta` (the facts as one ruled box, two to a row), `Lines` (the document chooses its columns; `amountColumn`; headings and grand total on pale gold bands; its lines banded white and gold by `paperStripe`, which every paper that lists lines uses, docs/adr/0039), `Signatures` (each signatory's name, their position under it when the document keeps one, and their role; with `signed`, the signature Company Details keeps for them above their line, `SignatureImage`, which a paper with its own signature boxes, as the check voucher has, uses too: trimmed to its ink (`trimSignature`) and drawn into the same box, three times as wide as high, on the middle of its line, at that signatory's size and place, so every signature reads alike; where the company module's `SERVICES.signatureSizes` lets whoever is signed in (the CEO), a dashed frame on hover or focus moves it (drag, Shift and arrows) and resizes it by its corner (drag, arrows) on every paper, and never prints, docs/adr/0045), `FitWidth` (a part wider than the paper, such as the payroll sheet's 22 columns, laid out as narrow as it goes and scaled down to fit across the paper, the same on screen, in print and in the PDF), and the papers' two text styles, `paperHeading` (gold tracked capitals on a band) and `paperLabel` (a box's grey label), both 11px, the house minimum |
| `src/parts.tsx` | Where on the paper the field being typed in lands (docs/adr/0042): `Fills` (`<Fills part="payee">` around a field or a group of them, taking no room) says which part of the paper the fields inside fill; `usePaperPart` (`part('payee')` spread on an element) says which part a paper's element is, and gives it `data-paper-active` only while a field that fills it has focus in a `Workspace`, so a paper anywhere else, printed or exported, is unchanged. `Meta`'s rows, `Lines`, `Signatures`' signatories and `Letterhead` (`no`, `date`) take part names. The stylesheet draws the mark, a ring in the papers' gold, on screen only, and Export PDF leaves it out |
| `src/output.ts` | `savePdf` and `printElement`: the element on screen is the one printed. Both read the paper's layout back from the marks it carries and do what that layout says; neither knows which paper it is. Export PDF is the print itself where the platform can print a page to a PDF (`files.printPdf`, the Windows app), and otherwise the paper drawn by `paper-pdf.ts` (docs/adr/0043, 0047) |
| `src/paper-pdf.ts` | `paperPdf`: a paper drawn into an A4 PDF as the browser laid it out: its text as text in the page's own fonts, embedded and selectable; its backgrounds and borders as shapes; its logo and signatures as its only pictures; an upright paper onto as many pages as it runs to (docs/adr/0047) |
| `src/platform.ts` | The `Platform` interface the app implements for Tauri and for the browser, and for the records in the cloud: who is signed in (`who`), signing out (`session`), the services the platform itself provides (`provides`: with a sign-in, core's `accounts` service, docs/adr/0033), Keystone's own updates (`updater`), live updates of the saved state where others save to it too (`watchState`: the store shows their saves as they are made and keeps a change of its own not saved yet on top), files, photos and the operating system. No part is handed it |

Tests in `test/`: the registry and services (`registry.test.ts`), the store (`runtime.test.ts`), the connector
shortcut (`shortcut.test.tsx`), the paper's marked part (`parts.test.tsx`), the update notice (`update-notice.test.tsx`), signing out (`session.test.tsx`), screens
coming soon (`coming-soon.test.tsx`) and a form marking the fields a refusal names (`form-card.test.tsx`), against an in-memory platform
(`fakes.ts`). `test/setup.ts` is the shared jsdom setup every
package's tests use (`vitest.shared.ts`).

When two parts need the same thing, it moves here (Keystone-specific) or to `@keystone/ui` (generic).
