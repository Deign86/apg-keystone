# @keystone/kit

The app runtime every module and connector builds on. It depends on `@keystone/core` and `@keystone/ui` only and knows
no module and no connector (`npm run check:boundaries`). Parts import it by name: `import { useModuleApp } from
'@keystone/kit'`.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `src/registry.ts` | `ModuleDef`, `ModuleApp`, and `buildRegistry`, which validates parts, keeps one provider per service, and leaves out anything broken, with a reason. `findService` looks a service up by its published name |
| `src/store.ts` | Holds the saved state, runs rules (`run`), writes `state.json` about 250ms after a burst of changes, never overwrites a damaged file until something changes, and runs modules' `onOpen` housekeeping |
| `src/context.tsx` | Hooks: `useKeystone` (the state, `ctx`, `run`, which returns the state after the rule, and the capabilities a screen may use: `files`, `photos`, `backup`; never the store or the platform), `useModuleApp` (`go`, `has`, `setDraft`, `service`), `useDraft`, `useOpenConnector` (opens a connected system's app under a 15-second deadline and says what happened), `usePhoto` |
| `src/Shell.tsx` | The rail, ⌘/Ctrl+number navigation, and one error boundary per screen |
| `src/ComingSoon.tsx` | `comingSoon`: a module as a screen not released yet (docs/adr/0017, 0019), marked "Soon" in the rail and "Coming soon" on its Home tile; its own screen shows faded behind a "Coming soon" notice, `inert` and hidden from screen readers. It keeps everything it contributes but its finding |
| `src/Session.tsx` | Signing out of the records in the cloud (docs/adr/0012): `SignOutButton` in the rail, and `IdleSignOut`, which asks "Still there?" a minute before and signs out once Keystone has sat unused for the session's `idleMs`. Nothing without `Platform.session` |
| `src/App.tsx` | The provider tree, used by the app's `main.tsx` and by its tests |
| `src/components.tsx` | Building blocks for screens: `LineEditor` (amounts, or `pricing="unit"` for quantity × unit price), `Workspace` (the form beside its document; side by side, the preview is pinned by default: it stays in view while the form scrolls, and a paper taller than the window scrolls in its own box. Pin preview, shown side by side only, unpins it to scroll with the page and show the whole paper; the choice is one for every screen's preview, kept with the drafts while Keystone is open), `FormCard`, `ConnectorShortcut` (a connected system's logo, which opens it, and its app's newer version under it, asked when it shows and every hour, docs/adr/0035), `Feed`, `BankAccountPicker` (a company bank account from the bank-accounts service, with Others… to add one and Remove…; a voucher's Bank and a statement's Pay into) |
| `src/paper.ts` | The paper, written once: an A4 page and its 12 mm print margin (`PAPER`, `PRINTABLE`, and the pixel sizes worked out from them), the marks a paper carries (`MARKS`), and one definition per layout (`UPRIGHT`, `LANDSCAPE_PAGE`, `TWO_UP`): its width, its `@page` rule, how it is fitted to the page before printing and put back after, and how its PDF page is set up. A kit test holds the print stylesheet to it |
| `src/documents.tsx` | Building blocks for printed documents. Three sheets, each a layout from `paper.ts`, always laid out at the paper's width and scaled down on screen to fit the column, so the preview, the print and the PDF are one layout: `Sheet` (upright, inside the margins), `LandscapePage` (the A4 on its side, for a sheet too wide to stand upright, printed scaled to one page) and `TwoUp` (the document twice on a whole A4, a copy on each half 6 mm in from the edges, with a dashed line across the middle to cut along). `Masthead` (the row a letterhead opens with: a logo and anything beside it, the title, number and date) and `Letterhead` (a masthead with the APG logo made for paper, `@keystone/ui/logo-paper.png`, and under it the company as Company Details keeps it); a paper with a letterhead of its own, as the check voucher has, composes `Masthead`. `Meta` (the facts as one ruled box, two to a row), `Lines` (the document chooses its columns; `amountColumn`; headings and grand total on pale gold bands), `Signatures` (each signatory's name, their position under it when the document keeps one, and their role), `FitWidth` (a part wider than the paper, such as the payroll sheet's 22 columns, laid out as narrow as it goes and scaled down to fit across the paper, the same on screen, in print and in the PDF), and the papers' two text styles, `paperHeading` (gold tracked capitals on a band) and `paperLabel` (a box's grey label), both 11px, the house minimum |
| `src/output.ts` | `savePdf` and `printElement`: the element on screen is the one printed. Both read the paper's layout back from the marks it carries and do what that layout says; neither knows which paper it is |
| `src/platform.ts` | The `Platform` interface the app implements for Tauri and for the browser, and for the records in the cloud: who is signed in (`who`), signing out (`session`), the services the platform itself provides (`provides`: with a sign-in, core's `accounts` service, docs/adr/0033), Keystone's own updates (`updater`), live updates of the saved state where others save to it too (`watchState`: the store shows their saves as they are made and keeps a change of its own not saved yet on top), files, photos and the operating system. No part is handed it |

Tests in `test/`: the registry and services (`registry.test.ts`), the store (`runtime.test.ts`), the connector
shortcut (`shortcut.test.tsx`), the update notice (`update-notice.test.tsx`), signing out (`session.test.tsx`) and screens
coming soon (`coming-soon.test.tsx`), against an in-memory platform
(`fakes.ts`). `test/setup.ts` is the shared jsdom setup every
package's tests use (`vitest.shared.ts`).

When two parts need the same thing, it moves here (Keystone-specific) or to `@keystone/ui` (generic).
