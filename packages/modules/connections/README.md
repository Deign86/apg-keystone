# Connections

## Owns
- A settings form for every connector, drawn from its `settings`. Secrets go to the OS keychain.
- Test again, and each connector's import button.
- Data: back up, restore, and reset to the sample data (each asks first).
- The list of parts that were left out, with their reasons.

## Contributes
Nothing.

## Needs
Nothing.

## Removing it
Connector cards lose their Settings button. Connectors keep their last saved settings, and imports stay available on
Home.
